Privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026. Short version: we collect your email address, use it only for Burn Rate launch news, and never sell it.

What we collect

Only the email address you type into the early-access form. Nothing else is asked for.

How it's handled

Signups are stored by our email provider, Kit (kit.com), under its own privacy policy. Kit sends you a confirmation email first, and you're only added to the list after you confirm (double opt-in).

What we send

Launch news about Burn Rate, and not often. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and one click takes you off the list.

What we don't do

We never sell or rent your email, and we don't share it with anyone except Kit, which sends the emails for us. This site uses no cookies, analytics or ad trackers. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses to run and secure the service (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Questions or deletion

Want your email deleted, or have a question? Write to hello@burnrategames.com and we'll take care of it.
